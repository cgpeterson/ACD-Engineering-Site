Mint Backup

Your files, safely copied. Automatically.

Mint Backup is a small Windows app that keeps an encrypted copy of one folder in storage you already own — Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or an external drive. Once it's set up, there's nothing to remember.

What it does

  • Backs up your chosen folder in the background a few minutes after you save a file, and every hour while your computer is on.
  • Keeps every version of your files from the last 90 days, so you can go back to an earlier copy or recover something you deleted.
  • Keeps working without internet: changes are held on your computer and uploaded when the connection comes back.
  • Encrypts everything on your computer before it is uploaded. Only you can open your backups.

Connecting Google Drive

When you choose Google Drive, Mint Backup asks Google for permission to see and manage only the files Mint Backup itself creates. It cannot see, read or change anything else in your Google Drive. Your encrypted backup is stored in a folder named MintBackup.

You can remove this permission at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections.
